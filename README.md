# Between Worlds: Lost Soul - The Game
A 2.5D dark urban fantasy narrative RPG blending <i>Darkest Dungeon</i>-inspired tactical turn-based combat with <i>Persona Series</i> character dialogue, interactions, and sprites to create a game that focuses on combat with a sanity meter. 
## Game Overview
Follow Lily Ly-Kadoya, an 18-year-old college freshman trying to maintain a normal life despite her origins from another world and the loss of her biological parents. Her fragile sense of normalcy shatters when she is pulled into the Mirror World—a realm infested with Demons, monstrous manifestations of raw human shame and regret.

When her adoptive mother, an AEGIS Agent named Angel, is overwhelmed while fighting a powerful Demon, Lily makes a desperate pact with a Demon of her own: Ink Spot. Lily will navigate as an AEGIS Agent fighting Demons with the help of her squadmates: Alvin, Eva, and Elliot.

## Roster & Character Systems
### Combat Resources & Armor
- PV (Perseverance) is the Will to Fight: If a character's PV reaches 0, their Masked Rider armor shatters, and they are forced to retreat. Even if Armor Integrity reaches 0%, their armor will remain stable so long as PV remains above 0. 
- AR (Armor Integrity) (Half-Life 2 style tiers):
  - Medium Armor (Alvin & Elliot): 60% damage reduction, remaining 40% bleeds into PV (100% max counter).
  - Light Armor (Eva): 40% damage reduction, remaining 60% bleeds into PV.
- HP (Health Points): Lily and Demons operate on a standard HP system.
  - To offset her unique mechanics, she has a passive regeneration of 3 HP at the start of her turn.
  - When Lily's HP reaches 0, Lily's Armor will violently attack Demons and knock her allies out of their armor before it reaches game over, where the game continues from a previous save file.

Lily has a Sanity Meter that increases at the start of her turn:
- 0-20 - Sane
- 21-80 - Stressed. Deals 10% more damage at the cost of lowering PV and AR recovery from 60% and 50% to 45% and 30%, respectively. 
- 81-100 - Manic. If the player chooses not to treat Lily's Sanity Meter, the PV and AR will continue to decrease by 3% for every encounter until PV and AR stop at 15% and 10%, respectively.
  - To treat her sanity, the player can choose among CDs from real-life artists.
